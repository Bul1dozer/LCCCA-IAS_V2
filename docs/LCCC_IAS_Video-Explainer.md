Create a complete, professional, screen-led training explainer video for the Life Changing Christian Church Academy Invoice Automation System (LCCA-IAS v2.0).

IMPORTANT:
The attached screenshots represent the actual current user interface of the system. Use them as the primary visual reference. The narration must describe what is visibly shown on the screenshots and the documented functionality of LCCA-IAS. Do not invent buttons, fields, workflows, capabilities, or screen elements that are not supported by the screenshots or official documentation.

AUDIENCE:
The video is for school administrators, finance staff, and other office personnel who may have little or no technical background.

OBJECTIVE:
Teach a first-time administrator how to confidently use LCCA-IAS for day-to-day school administration and financial operations, from logging in through monthly billing, payment recording, reporting, reconciliation, security, and logout.

TONE:
Professional, patient, reassuring, instructional, and conversational.
Explain the system as though you are personally training a new staff member sitting beside you.
Avoid technical jargon.
When a technical term is unavoidable, immediately explain it in ordinary language.

FORMAT:
Use two hosts.

Host 1 should act as the main trainer and system expert.
Host 2 should act as the administrator/student, asking realistic questions that a non-technical user would naturally ask.

The conversation must feel natural rather than like two people reading documentation.

SCREEN PRESENTATION:
Use the supplied screenshots as visual anchors.
Move through the system in the same logical order that an administrator would use it.
When a screenshot is displayed, the narration should identify the page, explain the visible controls, and explain what the administrator should do there.
Use visual callouts, highlights, zooms, cursor emphasis, and transitions where appropriate.
Do not visually highlight controls that do not exist.

VIDEO LENGTH:
Target approximately 25–30 minutes.
Do not rush important workflows merely to stay short.
The video may extend toward 35 minutes if necessary to properly explain important functions.
Do not allow the video to exceed 35 minutes unless absolutely necessary.
Do not compress the explanation so heavily that a first-time administrator cannot follow it.
